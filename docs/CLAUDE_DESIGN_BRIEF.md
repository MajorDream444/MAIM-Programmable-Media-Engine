# Claude Design Brief — MAIM Programmable Media Engine

## Assignment

Use the existing MAIM visual structure as the foundation for a reusable programmable-media component library and agent design system.

Do not redesign MAIM from scratch. Extend the system so agents can reliably produce films, lessons, trailers, course media, social derivatives, client promos, repo demos, and consistent brand endings.

## Existing Foundation

- black foundation
- gold authority
- Jamaican green accents
- champion blue highlights
- green · gold · blue tri-bar
- Archivo display type
- JetBrains Mono technical type
- crown coin seal as trust mark
- Major Dream signature / MD watermark
- premium editorial composition
- warm cinematic lighting
- human-first imagery
- minimal typography
- technology as a natural human tool

## Required Design Work

Create a coherent library specification for:

1. Founder-led documentary frames
2. Premium title and word reveals
3. ABC progression sequences
4. Doctrine cards and quote frames
5. Network and connection metaphors
6. Text-to-system transformations
7. Lesson chapter cards
8. Caption systems for 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16
9. Lower thirds and identity straps
10. Timeline and progression diagrams
11. Evidence, source, and archive cards
12. CTA and offer cards
13. Crown seal and signature lockups
14. End cards and powered-by signatures
15. YouTube, Shorts, course, and client-film derivatives
16. Contact sheets, review boards, and QA overlays

## Deliverables

Return:

- design principles
- component inventory
- component anatomy
- states and variants
- spacing and hierarchy rules
- motion behavior
- safe-area behavior
- responsive crop behavior
- audio interaction notes
- accessibility and readability requirements
- prohibited patterns
- export inventory
- token proposal
- provenance/source list
- implementation notes for Remotion

## Critical Constraints

- Humans remain the hero.
- Do not use generic AI robots or cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Do not invent official logo geometry.
- Do not make permanent color values canonical without sampling approved source assets.
- One primary motion idea per shot.
- Important words and marks require breathing room after resolution.
- Every component must explain when it should and should not be used.
- Motion recipes may be inspired by external references, but MAIM typography, colors, materials, and marks must remain original and source-controlled.

## Output Format

Organize the result as a design-system handoff suitable for committing to:

`design-system/`
`library/typography/`
`library/motion/`
`library/captions/`
`library/diagrams/`
`library/brand-signatures/`

End with a prioritized first-build list for the 30-second MAIM Welcome Film proof.
