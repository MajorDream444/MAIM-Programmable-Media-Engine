# Production Lifecycle

1. Record and preserve the original filename.
2. Upload the master to the correct Drive brand/year/slug folder.
3. Download a local working copy.
4. Ingest with `skills/ingest-maim-video`; verify `assets/raw.mp4`.
5. Transcribe with timestamps and correct proper nouns.
6. Map thesis, chapters, claims, assignments, and clips.
7. Approve the editorial plan before destructive cuts.
8. Edit the master, captions, motion graphics, and derivatives.
9. QA audio, frames, captions, spelling, safe areas, branding, claims, links, and endings.
10. Record founder approval or changes.
11. Package thumbnails, chapters, metadata, shorts, Substack packet, and provenance.
12. Publish only to approved destinations.
13. Record URLs, performance, and lessons.
14. Archive master, project, transcript, manifests, and approved derivatives.

`ingested` requires ffprobe evidence. `approved_to_edit` requires an accepted plan. `awaiting_review` requires technical QA. `approved_to_publish` requires founder/delegate approval. `published` requires destination URLs.
